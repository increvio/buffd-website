The muscle growth zone

How the zone works.

The zone shows how much of this week's possible muscle growth you're actually getting. It isn't an average. It works like a chain: if a basic is missing, the whole number drops.

Zone = Training × Calories × Protein × Sleep × Progression × Consistency

Training and calories are the basics. Protein, sleep, progression and consistency are brakes: each one can only cost you part of your growth.

Under 50%Barely building 50 to 79%On the way 80% and upIn the zone
  • No training, no growth.

    More hard sets per week bring more muscle, with smaller gains the more you add. Food and protein can't replace the training itself.

    Pelland et al. 2026, Sports Medicine, 67 studies · Schoenfeld et al. 2017, Journal of Sports Sciences
  • A big calorie deficit stalls growth.

    In a meta-analysis, gains in lean mass stopped at around 500 kcal a day below needs, on average.

    Murphy & Koehler 2022, Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports
  • Protein helps up to about 1.6 g per kg.

    Above roughly 1.6 g per kg of body weight a day, extra protein showed no further benefit. The exact cut-off is uncertain.

    Morton et al. 2018, British Journal of Sports Medicine, 49 trials

What nobody has measured yet. No long-term study compares muscle growth with sleep length, and no study measures what a week without progress costs. Those parts of the zone are our best estimates, not measured values.

Questions

What is Buffd?

An iPhone app with everything you need to build muscle. You log food and workouts, and the app shows how close your week is to real muscle growth and which habit is holding you back.

Who is it for?

Anyone 18 or older who lifts and wants to build muscle, especially if you're new to the gym and don't know why you're not getting bigger or stronger.

What is the muscle growth zone?

One number for your week. It combines training, calories, protein, sleep, progression and consistency. Missing training or eating far too little pulls it down the most, because without them muscles don't grow.

Where does my sleep come from?

From Apple Health if you allow it. Otherwise you enter it yourself in the morning.

Can I use my own training plan?

Yes. Follow the plan in the app, change it, or set up your own.

What does it cost?

Buffd is a subscription. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free, then costs $49.99 a year in the US. Monthly is $11.99. You can cancel anytime in your iPhone settings, and if you cancel during the free week, you pay nothing.

Is Buffd a medical app?

No. Buffd is a wellness app for adults. It doesn't diagnose anything and doesn't replace medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to your diet or training.
