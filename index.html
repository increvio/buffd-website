Questions

What is Buffd?

An app for building muscle. It tells you what to train, logs your sets and food, and shows what holds your gains back.

Who is it for?

Beginners and intermediate lifters, 16 and older. At the gym or at home.

What does the chart show?

Six things for your week: training, progression, protein, calories, sleep and consistency. Your weakest one comes first, with the next step.

Can I use my own training plan?

Yes. Use ours, pick a split, swap exercises or add your own.

Where does my sleep come from?

From Apple Health, if you allow it.

What does it cost?

Yearly: 7 days free, then €39.99 a year (€3.33 a month). Monthly: €9.99, no free days. Cancel anytime. Cancel within the 7 free days and you pay nothing.

Is Buffd a medical app?

No. It's a wellness app for ages 16 and up. It doesn't replace a doctor.
