Questions

What does it cost?

Yearly: 7 days free, then €39.99 a year (€3.33 a month). Monthly: €9.99, no free week. The App Store shows the price for your country.

How do I cancel?

iPhone settings, your name, Subscriptions, Buffd. Cancel in the free week and you pay nothing.

Do I need an account?

No. Your data stays on your iPhone. With an account, you get it back on a new phone. Stored in Frankfurt (EU).

Where does my sleep come from?

From Apple Health, if you allow it. Or you add it in the morning.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile, Data & privacy, "Delete account and all data". Everything is gone, on the server and your iPhone.

Which languages?

English and German.
